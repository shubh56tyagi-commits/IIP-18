About Our Mission

Small actions today can create a better world tomorrow.

Wildlife and nature are essential for life on Earth. Animals, plants, forests and rivers maintain the balance of our ecosystem. However, deforestation, pollution, climate change and illegal hunting threaten many species.

WildGuard spreads awareness about wildlife protection and environmental conservation. Our goal is to encourage everyone to protect our planet.

Endangered Wildlife

Learn about amazing animals and the importance of protecting their natural habitats.

Endangered

Bengal Tiger

Tigers help maintain the balance of forest ecosystems. Habitat loss and poaching threaten their survival.

Endangered

Elephant

Elephants help spread seeds and support healthy ecosystems. Habitat loss and illegal ivory trade threaten them.

At Risk

Rhinoceros

Several rhinoceros species face threats from poaching and habitat destruction. Conservation helps protect their future.
